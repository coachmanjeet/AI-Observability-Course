# Skill: spoiled or damaged food

Use when a customer reports spoiled or damaged perishables.

1. Look up the order, the refund policy and the photo.
2. If the photo was taken more than 24 hours after delivery, say:
   "Spoiled items need a photo within 24 hours of delivery."
   Offer to escalate.
3. Otherwise refund up to the approval limit, or escalate above it.
